# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA Lead

### Role Summary
QA Leads own the overall quality strategy for projects, coordinate testing efforts, and ensure that all work meets the Definition of Done. They work closely with developers and product managers to maintain high quality standards and catch issues early.

### Responsibilities
- Define and maintain the quality strategy and testing approach
- Coordinate test planning, execution, and result tracking
- Triage and prioritize bugs and quality issues
- Ensure acceptance criteria and Definition of Done are met before release
- Mentor developers and testers on quality best practices
- Review test coverage and recommend improvements
- Validate that features meet user expectations and quality standards

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, high-quality features to users
- Reduce post-release defects and support burden
- Build a quality-first culture across the team
- Enable faster, more confident releases

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups with development team
- Test status reports and quality metrics
- Collaboration with Product Manager on acceptance criteria
- Release readiness assessments with Project Manager

### Interactions
- **Partners with Developers** on test coverage, test case design, and quality reviews
- **Aligns with Project Managers** on release criteria and quality gates
- **Works with Product Managers** to clarify acceptance criteria and user scenarios
- **Advises DevOps Engineers** on deployment validation and smoke testing

---

## Security Engineer

### Role Summary
Security Engineers drive security assessments, threat modeling, and vulnerability mitigation for features and releases. They embed security into the development process and help the team build secure, defensible systems.

### Responsibilities
- Perform threat modeling and security assessments for new features
- Review code and infrastructure for security vulnerabilities
- Define and maintain security requirements and standards
- Advise on secure design patterns and technologies
- Participate in security incident response and post-mortems
- Coordinate security scanning and compliance checks in CI/CD
- Educate developers on secure coding practices

### Goals
- Prevent security breaches and data exposure
- Embed security early in the development lifecycle
- Maintain regulatory compliance and audit readiness
- Build team confidence in the security posture of releases

### Typical Communication
- Security design reviews and threat modeling sessions
- Vulnerability reports and remediation plans
- Security guidance and best practices documentation
- Incident response coordination

### Interactions
- **Works with Developers** during design and implementation to ensure secure coding
- **Consults with Product Managers** on security requirements and user privacy
- **Coordinates with Project Managers** on security milestones and risk escalation
- **Partners with DevOps Engineers** on infrastructure security and compliance automation

---

## UX Designer

### Role Summary
UX Designers own the user experience and interaction design, creating intuitive and usable interfaces. They advocate for user needs throughout the project, conduct user research, and iterate designs based on feedback.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct user research and usability testing
- Create wireframes, mockups, and design specifications
- Define user flows and interaction patterns
- Ensure consistency with design system and brand standards
- Iterate designs based on user feedback and testing results
- Collaborate with developers on feasibility and implementation
- Advocate for user needs and accessibility standards
- Gather and synthesize feedback from stakeholders and end users

### Goals
- Create intuitive, delightful user experiences
- Reduce user friction and support burden
- Increase user adoption and satisfaction
- Ensure products are accessible to all users

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and feedback sessions
- User research reports and insights
- Design specifications and design system updates
- Collaboration with developers on implementation details
- Stakeholder demos and feedback gathering

### Interactions
- **Collaborates with Product Managers** on feature requirements and user scenarios
- **Works with Developers** to ensure design feasibility and quality implementation
- **Gathers feedback from Stakeholder Advocate** to represent customer voice
- **Aligns with QA Lead** on acceptance criteria related to usability

---

## DevOps Engineer

### Role Summary
DevOps Engineers maintain CI/CD systems, infrastructure as code, and release automation. They enable teams to build, test, and deploy software safely and efficiently, while maintaining observability and reliability.

### Responsibilities
- Design and maintain CI/CD pipelines and automation
- Manage infrastructure as code and cloud resources
- Implement security scanning and compliance checks in deployment
- Monitor system health, performance, and incidents
- Create and maintain deployment documentation and runbooks
- Coach teams on observability, logging, and debugging
- Manage release processes and rollback procedures
- Optimize build times and reduce deployment friction

### Goals
- Enable fast, reliable, safe deployments
- Maintain high system availability and performance
- Reduce mean time to recovery (MTTR) for incidents
- Empower teams with self-service deployment capabilities

### Typical Communication
- CI/CD pipeline status and deployment reports
- Infrastructure and deployment documentation
- Incident alerts and post-mortem coordination
- Guidance on observability and debugging

### Interactions
- **Supports Developers** in building, testing, and deploying software
- **Collaborates with QA Lead** on deployment validation and smoke testing
- **Informs Project Managers** of deployment status and release readiness
- **Works with Security Engineers** on infrastructure security and compliance automation

---

## Stakeholder Advocate

### Role Summary
Stakeholder Advocates ensure that the voice of stakeholders and customers is represented throughout the project lifecycle. They gather feedback, escalate needs and concerns, and help the team stay aligned with business and user priorities.

### Responsibilities
- Gather and synthesize feedback from stakeholders and end users
- Represent stakeholder perspectives in planning and prioritization
- Escalate risks, blockers, and unmet needs to leadership
- Track stakeholder satisfaction and feedback trends
- Facilitate communication between product team and stakeholders
- Relay insights from retrospectives and customer interactions
- Help define success criteria aligned with stakeholder expectations
- Bridge gaps between technical teams and business/customer needs

### Goals
- Ensure customer voice is heard and prioritized
- Reduce misalignment between delivery and stakeholder expectations
- Enable data-driven decision making based on stakeholder feedback
- Increase stakeholder satisfaction and trust

### Typical Communication
- Stakeholder feedback reports and summaries
- Regular updates to Product and Project Managers
- Escalation notifications and risk reports
- Feedback collection and analysis

### Interactions
- **Communicates with Product Managers** on customer needs and market feedback
- **Works with Project Managers** to escalate blockers and address risk
- **Gathers feedback from UX Designers** on user experience insights
- **Relays insights** from retrospectives and lessons learned across the team
